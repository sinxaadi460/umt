Kaun sa certificate kaun deta hai? (3 hisse)

Part A — Board / University wale

Kya: School class (Nursery–12), degree (B.A, B.Com, BCA, M.A...), B.Ed / D.El.Ed, ITI, medical courses.

Kaun issue karega: Sirf aap — affiliated school / college — apne naam, mohar aur sign se.

Portal kya deta hai: Sirf format (design), Excel bulk generate, QR aur online verification. Portal ka naam certificate par nahi aata.

Part B — Institute ke naam se

Kya: PG / Master diploma, NTT, SAP / AutoCAD jaise brand naam, security guard, electrician, fire safety, technical diploma.

Kaun issue karega: Aapka institute, apne naam / mohar / sign se, apni zimmedari par.

Portal kya deta hai: Part A jaisa hi — sirf format + verification.

Part C — Portal Skill Certificate

Kya: Private skill training — computer, typing, Tally, tailoring, beautician, spoken English, agriculture skills...

Kaun issue karega: Portal se generate hota hai (aapki Branding setting ke hisaab se naam).

Below, chhota note: "Private skill-training certificate, kisi University / Board ki degree nahi."

Generate karne ka tareeka teeno me same hai — Generate Documents ya Bulk Excel. Farq sirf itna hai ki certificate par kiska naam chhapega aur zimmedari kiski hai.
